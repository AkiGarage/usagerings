UsageRings needs CodexBar

UsageRings reads your AI usage through the free CodexBar app (MIT license).

1. Install CodexBar: https://github.com/steipete/CodexBar/releases/latest
2. Put CodexBar.app in the Applications folder and sign in to your AI services there.
3. Drag UsageRings.app to Applications and open it.

UsageRingsは無料のCodexBarアプリを通して使用量を読み取ります。
1. CodexBarをインストール: https://github.com/steipete/CodexBar/releases/latest
2. CodexBar.appを「アプリケーション」に入れ、各AIサービスにログイン
3. UsageRings.appを「アプリケーション」へドラッグして起動
